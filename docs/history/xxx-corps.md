## Key facts

- XXX Corps started its attack from Joe's Bridge at 14:35 on 17 Sep. Source: PLAN §14.2 (confirmed), TODO(history). The scenario timeline (PLAN §5.5) uses 14:30 for the off-map signal.
- The XXX Corps axis was called "Club Route". Source: PLAN §14.2 (confirmed), TODO(history).
- Household Cavalry reached Grave at 08:20 on 19 Sep; the Grenadier Group arrived about 10:00; the first Allied attack on the Nijmegen bridges was at 15:15. Source: [US Army CMH, ch. 7][cmh7].
- Grenadier Guards tanks crossed the Nijmegen road bridge at about 19:10–19:15 on 20 Sep and linked up with 3rd Battalion, 504th PIR. Source: [US Army CMH, ch. 8][cmh8].
- The 4th Dorsets belonged to 130th Brigade, not 214th Brigade. Source: [battle-of-arnhem.com][dorsets].
- XXX Corps never reached Arnhem. Source: PLAN §5.6, TODO(history).
