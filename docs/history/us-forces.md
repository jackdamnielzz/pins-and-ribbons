## Key facts

- 504th PIR dropped on DZ O (Overasselt); E Company dropped on a separate zone next to the Grave bridge. Source: PLAN §5.2, TODO(history).
- 82nd Airborne got only about half its gliders on 19 Sep. Source: PLAN §5.5, TODO(history).
- On 18 Sep Korps Feldt (406th Division) attacked DZ N and Mook from the Reichswald; on 20 Sep II. Fallschirmkorps attacked with four Kampfgruppen. Source: [Warfare History Network][groesbeek]. See [german-forces.md](german-forces.md).
- First Allied attack on the Nijmegen bridges: 15:15 on 19 Sep. Source: [US Army CMH, ch. 7][cmh7].
- 3rd Battalion, 504th PIR crossed the Waal in 26 canvas boats at 15:00 on 20 Sep. Grenadier Guards tanks crossed the Nijmegen road bridge at about 19:10 and linked up with them. Source: PLAN §5.6 and §14.2 (confirmed); bridge time from [US Army CMH, ch. 8][cmh8] (about 19:10–19:15).
