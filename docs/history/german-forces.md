## Full campaign only

- schwere Panzer-Abteilung 506 (King Tigers) arrives at Zevenaar on the night of 23/24 September: its 2nd company goes to Elst and its 3rd company to the Oosterbeek perimeter. Source: [506th Heavy Panzer Battalion][spz506].

## Key facts

- **Arnhem bridge guard:** only a few bridge keepers, not a light infantry unit. No unit was ordered to the bridge until Brinkmann late on 17 Sep. Source: [battle-of-arnhem.com][bridgeguard].
- **Kampfgruppe Harder:** part of KG Spindler, split off on 18 Sep, held Oosterbeek station to the Rhine. It did not counter-attack the Arnhem bridge. Source: [Battle of Arnhem][wiki-arnhem].
- **Kampfgruppe Knaust:** arrived about 04:00 on 18 Sep (not 19 Sep) with 8–10 old Panzer III/IV. Source: [Weapons and Warfare][knaust].
- **Tigers:** the first Tigers (Kompanie Hummel) arrived on 19 Sep; sPzAbt 506 only from the night of 23/24 Sep. Source: [506th Heavy Panzer Battalion][spz506].
- **Groesbeek attacks:** Korps Feldt (406th Division) on 18 Sep; II. Fallschirmkorps with four Kampfgruppen on 20 Sep (not KG Becker alone). Source: [Warfare History Network][groesbeek].
- **10th SS** reached Nijmegen using the Pannerden ferry. Source: PLAN §14.2 (confirmed), TODO(history).
- **Nijmegen road bridge:** prepared for demolition, but Model withheld permission to blow it. Source: [US Army CMH, ch. 8][cmh8].
- **Nijmegen rail bridge:** intact and prepared for demolition. Source: PLAN §5.1, TODO(history).
- **Arnhem pontoon bridge:** centre sections removed by the Germans; not crossable. Source: PLAN §5.1, TODO(history).
- **German losses** for the operation are disputed: about 3,300 by German reports, higher by later estimates. Source: PLAN §5.6, TODO(history).

## Design decisions (PLAN §14.3)

These were not settled by sources. They are final for version 1.0 and can be revised if better evidence turns up.

| Open point | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Ranks and first names of Krafft, Harder and Henke | Show surname only, with rank only where sources agree: "SS-Sturmbannführer Krafft", "Kampfgruppe Harder", "Oberst Henke". No first names in the game. |
| Kampfgruppe Melitz (Nijmegen) | Left out. Its men are folded into Kampfgruppe Henke. |
| Panthers at Elst | Not used. German armour on the Island is Panzer IV (10th SS) and, from 24 September, King Tigers of sPzAbt 506. |
| Nebelwerfers | One German rocket-launcher battery (artillery type, range 8 hexes) joins the Arnhem–Oosterbeek fighting from 20 September. |
| Time zones in German sources | All game times are stored in British time (UTC+1). Times taken from German sources are converted by subtracting one hour. |

## Content rule (PLAN §14.4)

Balkenkreuz yes; swastika and SS runes no, including in Germany (§86a StGB). This is a design rule, not legal advice; confirm with a games lawyer before release.
