## Time

- Game times are British time (UTC+1). German and Dutch clocks were one hour ahead until 2 October 1944. Source: [timeanddate][time].
- Design decision (PLAN §14.3): all game times are stored in British time. Times taken from German sources are converted by subtracting one hour.

## Maps and grid

- Allied maps used the Nord de Guerre grid on GSGS 2541 (1:100,000) and GSGS 4427 (1:25,000) sheets. Source: [Library of Congress][loc].

## Unit symbols

- Use the 1943 FM 21-30 conventional signs. NATO frame symbols are post-war and are not used. Source: [FM 21-30 (1943)][fm2130].

## Names and ranks (design decision, PLAN §14.3)

Show surname only, with rank only where sources agree: "SS-Sturmbannführer Krafft", "Kampfgruppe Harder", "Oberst Henke". No first names in the game.

## Content rule (PLAN §14.4)

Balkenkreuz yes; swastika and SS runes no, including in Germany (§86a StGB). This is a design rule, not legal advice; confirm with a games lawyer before release.

## Sources and process (PLAN §14)

- On 15 October 2026 four independent research passes checked the plan against archive and reference sources: British and Polish forces, US 82nd Airborne and XXX Corps, German forces, and tactics, terminology and conditions.
